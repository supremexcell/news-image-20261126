## Heroes

### 1. New tariffs on 14 partners; Nasdaq dives 4%
- The Trump administration on Wednesday proposed new tariffs on 14 major
  trading partners to combat what it calls structural excess industrial
  capacity, with the highest rates to hit China (20% excess-capacity
  levies, on top of roughly 25% duties from Trump's first term).
- The Nasdaq plunged about 4% intraday Wednesday as the AI selloff
  deepened, then recovered most of the loss: close 23,585.80, −0.62%.
  The Nasdaq 100 briefly fell 20% from its record closing high, flirting
  with bear-market territory; it closed −0.64% at 26,046.90. The Dow
  recovered from about −1,100 points to +0.33% at 50,182.95; the S&P 500
  lost 0.25% to 7,182.78. Nvidia fell 3.9% to $149.30.
- The Fed's preferred inflation gauge, PCE, came in at 3.8% in October.
- Sources: Reuters via Kitco, LA Post, NY Post, BBN Times, 25–26 Nov.

### 2. Kim: spread tactical nuclear weapons wider
- North Korean leader Kim Jong Un said his country should focus on
  deploying tactical nuclear weapons across multiple domains and that its
  nuclear material production capacity was being systematically expanded,
  state media KCNA reported on Thursday. Kim spoke during an inspection
  of nuclear material production and weapons research facilities, and
  said North Korea's status as a nuclear weapons state was "absolutely
  inviolable".
- Sources: KCNA via Reuters, 26 Nov.

## Grid

### 3. Ukraine hits air base; 5 planes damaged
- Ukrainian forces struck the Savasleyka air base in the Nizhny Novgorod
  region east of Moscow, damaging five planes used to launch missiles and
  bombs at Ukraine, the Ukrainian General Staff said on Wednesday. Russia
  did not immediately comment.
- Sources: Reuters, 26 Nov.

### 4. 31 killed in fuel-truck fire in Giza
- Thirty-one people were killed and 21 wounded in a fire after a vehicle
  carrying petroleum products overturned on a major ring road in Egypt,
  authorities said on Wednesday. The fire broke out above a tunnel in
  Giza's Al-Warraq district; preliminary estimates found 27 vehicles had
  been charred.
- Sources: Reuters, 26 Nov.

### 5. Murmansk governor held on graft charges
- The governor of Russia's strategically important Murmansk region was
  placed in pre-trial custody for two months on Wednesday in a major
  corruption investigation, a Moscow court said. Andrei Chibis is the
  first serving regional head to be arrested on corruption charges since
  the start of Russia's war in Ukraine. Murmansk is Russia's largest
  Arctic port and home to its Northern Fleet.
- Sources: Reuters, 26 Nov.

### 6. Second judge blocks birthright curbs
- A second US judge on Wednesday blocked enforcement of President Donald
  Trump's executive order limiting the number of people eligible for
  birthright citizenship, which Trump signed after the US Supreme Court
  rejected his initial effort. US District Judge Christopher Cooper in
  Washington, DC, issued an injunction in a class action filed on behalf
  of children who would be denied citizenship under the August order.
- Sources: Reuters, 26 Nov.

### 7. 34 nations: stop the riot gas, Russia
- Officials from 34 countries and the EU called on Russia on Wednesday
  to stop using riot-control gas on the battlefield, after the global
  chemical weapons watchdog confirmed that Ukraine had found traces of
  tear gas in a trench Kyiv said was hit with a Russian grenade. The
  joint statement was issued in response to a Ukrainian report circulated
  to the Organisation for the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons in The Hague.
- Sources: Reuters, 26 Nov.

### 8. Judges: drop one Netanyahu bribery case
- Israeli judges on Wednesday recommended that prosecutors drop one of
  three long-running cases against Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and
  reconsider another alleging he granted favours in exchange for positive
  media coverage. Netanyahu was indicted in 2019 on charges of bribery,
  fraud and breach of trust, denies the charges and has pleaded not
  guilty; his trial began in 2020.
- Sources: Reuters, 26 Nov.

### 9. Russian missiles hit Kyiv shopping centre
- Russian forces attacked the Ukrainian capital Kyiv with ballistic
  missiles late on Wednesday, striking a shopping centre and two other
  buildings, Mayor Vitali Klitschko said. Reuters witnesses reported two
  series of loud explosions in the city.
- Sources: Reuters, 26 Nov.

### 10. Puerto Rico's Barreto takes the crown
- Jennifer Barreto of Puerto Rico was crowned Miss Universe 2026 on
  Tuesday 24 Nov at the Coliseo de Puerto Rico in San Juan, becoming the
  first married woman and first mother to win the pageant, and Puerto
  Rico's sixth winner, its first since 2006. Reported widely 25 Nov.
- Sources: CNN, People, USA Today, 25 Nov.
